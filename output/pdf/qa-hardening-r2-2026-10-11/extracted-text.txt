Source and targets. The UCI extension of Z-Alizadeh Sani contains 303 records [1]. Cath supplies the overall
CAD label; LAD, LCX and RCA supply vessel status. All four angiography columns are excluded from every task,
leaving 55 predictors. Source hashes and frozen row identities are saved.
Preprocessing. Numeric median imputation and scaling, plus categorical imputation and one-hot encoding, are
refitted within each training partition. Inference preserves the fitted values and reports unseen categories. Original
source spelling is retained in the encoder.

The five availability groups are an explicit experiment definition informed by the source categories. Laboratory and echo are split
operationally; the dataset does not establish a temporal care pathway. Verified units are used only where supported by source
evidence [1,2].
Nested selection (V2). Five shared CAD-stratified outer folds evaluate the entire selection procedure. Three
target-stratified inner folds select C (0.01, 0.1, 1, 10), unweighted/balanced logistic fitting, and optional sigmoid
calibration by pooled inner log loss. Calibrated candidates use further cross-fitted training scores; outer validation
records are excluded from fitting and calibration [4,5].
Operating rule. The selected candidate maximizes inner out-of-fold F1 over thresholds 0.05-0.95 at 0.01 steps.
Ties favor proximity to 0.5, then the higher point. Outer classification metrics use the threshold fitted without each
validation record. AUC and Brier use probabilities, independent of this classification threshold [6].
Development audit: 0 missing cells; 0 duplicated input rows; 1 CAD/vessel label discrepancy preserved. V2
followed inspection of V1 development results, so development evidence includes experiment-selection
uncertainty.

Reference geometry. Official BodyParts3D memberships define heart FMA7088, LAD FMA3862, LCX FMA3895
and RCA FMA50039 [3]. Source OBJ parts are merged, triangulated and transformed into selectable Three.js
groups [7]. Provenance retains hashes, memberships and modifications.
Visual semantics. The target selects matching geometry, probability and explanation. Overview, opposite view,
mesh-bound focus and isolation complement rotate/zoom and the surface toggle. Isolation restores the chosen
surface state. Probability colors the whole vessel; no patient geometry or lesion coordinates are inferred.
Grouped interventional linear SHAP (log-odds)
For encoded values z, training means mu, coefficients beta and intercept b:
reference = b + sum(beta[j] * mu[j])
contribution[j] = beta[j] * (z[j] - mu[j])
score = reference + sum(contribution[j])
probability = sigmoid(score)
Encoded terms are grouped into raw inputs; calibration slope/offset enter coefficients/intercept. The waterfall
shows reference, six largest terms, residual and final score. Sigmoid applies at the endpoint; sigmoid(reference) is
not mean predicted probability.
Actual SHAP LinearExplainer with an Independent masker verifies the decomposition [9] using all encoded
training-fold rows. Masks break feature dependencies and may form invalid one-hot states. Terms are
interventional model log-odds, not conditional explanations or percentage-point probability changes.
Correlation-aware explanations can assign different credit. Terms show model associations; input edits explore
fitted-model sensitivity, not treatment effects or clinical interventions.
Verification and interaction
The compact export matched all 4,840 saved development probabilities exactly. It retains eight introductory cases plus the complete
development audit. Contributions are calculated lazily from the same 100 profile/target/fold models; labels never enter inference.
The separate complete-cohort actual-SHAP certificate passed 4,840 explanations across 100 models; maximum grouped-term error
5.55e-17 and reconstructed score error 5.33e-15, tolerance 1e-12. Its Independent background excludes the record, the entire outer
fold and every final holdout row. The original 160-explanation certificate remains scoped to the eight-case roster. These checks verify
the specified decomposition, not clinical explanation utility.
Audit selections open the anatomy, score waterfall and five-profile matrix. Outcome-aware selection is disclosed; label reveal is
separate from inference. Edits disable matching outcome comparison; unlabelled inputs use frozen full-development models.
AI software checks, 11 October. The fresh six-task review found stale case context and false alarms described as misses; corrections
were browser-checked. Five earlier case/profile failure, retry and keyboard checks passed on 11 October. Two synthetic delayed
HTTP503 checks confirmed old prediction errors cannot replace a newer case or release prediction controls during its load; the new
case then completes normally. Five review tasks completed; the sixth evidenced rotation, zoom and target mapping with an unretained
focus/isolation screenshot. These bounded AI-operated checks do not measure human comprehension or usability.

Limits
Small dataset; no external or prospective validation. A bounded cohort search admitted no full-55-input source: public AngioCAD lacks
the examination and ECG groups [10]. LCX/RCA have limited discrimination and a large false-positive cost. Intervals omit refitting
uncertainty. Independent targets can disagree. Generic anatomy cannot locate plaque; edits lack outcome labels.
Reproduce and verify
Run npm ci, python3 server.py; open http://127.0.0.1:8766/. Python standard library or the static browser performs
inference. Ordinary WebGL renders anatomy. docs/cohort-audit.md gives audit and pinned CPU verification commands.
Measured local runtime, 11 October. Saved pre-watchdog Chromium captures on this M5, 683 x 760, DPR 2; idle/action 10/10s:
58.8/58.6 foreground render callbacks/s. 8 completed actions: 6.9-19.7ms to DOM update; longest callback interval 264.2ms. These
narrow main-thread render-submission samples exclude GPU completion, physical-display latency, hosted speed, human usability and
second-device evidence. Safari rendering has not passed.
