A risk number becomes more useful when you can examine which artery it belongs to, which measurements drove it, and where the model made a mistake. Lumen connects these pieces in one working research application. It predicts overall coronary artery disease and three recorded vessel labels from clinical measurements, then maps the vessel estimates onto named reference anatomy. The geometry comes from BodyParts3D. It is a generic heart, not patient imaging, and its colors do not locate plaque or a lesion. This demonstration uses public development records and keeps educational limitations visible.
