Lumen also asks what predictive evidence survives when fewer input groups are available. This selector switches among history, examination, ECG, laboratory and echo profiles. Each reduced profile was independently refitted inside the same validation folds; the application is not just filling an entire missing group with defaults. Predictions, required inputs, artery colors, explanations and measured metrics update together. More recorded inputs did not uniformly improve development scores. All five comparisons remain visible. These are exploratory availability experiments. They do not establish the benefit, cost, or clinical order of obtaining a test.
