The source dataset contains 303 records. All four outcome columns are removed from every input set. A fixed split reserves 61 records for the final evaluation. On the remaining 242 records, five outer folds evaluate a complete training procedure. Three inner folds select regularization, class weighting and optional sigmoid calibration by log loss. Research thresholds are selected using inner predictions, before each outer validation fold is scored. The final full-input procedure was frozen before the held-out evaluation. Training runs on the CPU, and the live predictor uses portable coefficients, scalers, imputers and encoders.
